          Every API call in the 7-step flow (`Register`, `CheckStatusAndCorridor`, `ExecuteSenderKyc`, `Beneficiary/requirements`, `Providers`, `Types`, `Beneficiary`, `Exchange/rates`, `InitiateHostedTransaction`, `CompleteHostedPayment`) is logged below:

          Proposed 7-Step Olowo × MITO Integration Architecture

          1. Step 1 — User Registration & Sign In (`/register` & `/login`): At creation, check user by email; after creation, identify user by userId. Basic Olowo registration fields are collected here; full user info is submitted to POST /api/v1/mito/Users only when collected in Step 3.
          2. Step 2 — Rate Check & Immediate Rate Lock (`/remittances/create`): Default corridor GBP (UK — GBR) → NGN (Nigeria — NGA). Calls GET /api/v1/mito/Exchange/corridors & GET /api/v1/mito/Exchange/affiliateRates. Clicking 🔒 Lock Rate & Continue to checkout immediately locks the rate via POST /api/v1/mito/Exchange/rates and starts the side expiry countdown timer (redirecting back to Step 2 if expired).
          3. Step 3 — Sender Info & MITO KYC (Shown ONLY for New User or Changed Details): Once a user's KYC passes (PASSED), repeat KYC is skipped and Sender forms are not shown for existing verified senders unless their address or other details change.
          4. Step 4 — Full-Screen Beneficiary Selection / Creation: Existing saved beneficiaries are shown by default; the New Beneficiary creation form is shown only for a new user or when clicking Create new beneficiary +. Submits via POST /api/v1/mito/Beneficiary.
          5. Step 5 — Summary & Confirm: Reviews transfer, sender, beneficiary, and redirectUrl (/remittances/callback).
          6. Step 6 — Initiate Hosted Transaction & Live MITO Payment Page: Calls POST /api/v1/mito/Transactions/InitiateHostedTransaction and loads only requiredAction.payment.link inside the live embedded MITO Payment Checkout page.
          7. Step 7 — Redirect URL Payment Result (`GET /remittances/callback`): Captures and handles all 6 MITO payment cases (READYTOPROCESS / Success in Green, AWAITINGPAYMENT & INREVIEW in Amber, and CANCELLED, EXPIRED, & FAILED in Red without ever saying "You paid" or "Recipient receives" when payment did not complete).
